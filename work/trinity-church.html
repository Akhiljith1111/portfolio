Case study · Community web app

Trinity Marthoma Church

A community web app for a parish with weekly services, announcements, events, a gallery, prayer requests and a member portal.

Role
Full build
Built for
Trinity Marthoma Church
Type
Community website
Stack
Next.js · Tailwind

The problem

Schedules and announcements lived on notice boards and forwarded messages, so it was easy to miss what was happening that week.

The parish needed a single, phone-friendly place for everything: services, events, photos, and a way to reach out.

What I built

Content

Services & announcements

Weekly service schedules and announcements, easy to update and easy to read on a phone.

Community

Event calendar & gallery

An events calendar plus a gallery so members can catch up on what they missed.

Engagement

Online prayer requests

A simple form so members can submit prayer requests from anywhere.

Members

Member information portal

A portal for member information, replacing scattered paper records.

Outcome

One mobile-friendly home for the whole parish community.
  • Services, events, gallery and prayer requests in one place
  • Responsive design built for phones first

Tech used

Next.jsTailwind CSSREST APIsResponsive design

Takeaway

"For community sites, mobile-first isn't a nice-to-have. That's where everyone already is."
